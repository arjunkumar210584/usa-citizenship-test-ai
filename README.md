# Privacy Policy

**USA Citizenship Test AI**
Effective date: October 14, 2026
Package name: usa.citizenship.test.ai

USA Citizenship Test AI ("the app") is an independent educational tool for practicing the U.S. civics test and a naturalization interview. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by USCIS or any U.S. government agency.

This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and how you can delete it.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores study data on your device, including:

- quiz and mock-exam progress, scores, and spaced-repetition review state
- study settings, reminders, and language choice
- daily free-feature usage counts
- N-400 practice answers, which are encrypted on the device

This information stays on the device unless you turn on a cloud feature described below or you delete it. Android backup and device transfer of app data are turned off.

## Account information

You can sign in with Google or continue without an account.

- **Google sign-in:** we receive your Google account identifier and the name and email address Google provides, through Firebase Authentication.
- **Continue without an account:** Firebase creates an anonymous user ID. We do not receive your name or email.

We use the account only to keep you signed in, to apply your settings, and to delete cloud data when you ask.

## Microphone and voice

The app uses the microphone only while you start a voice feature, such as speaking an answer, the AI interview, or the live voice coach. The microphone is not used in the background.

- Speech is converted to text on the device or by the device's speech service.
- If you have turned on Cloud AI, the resulting text (and, for the live voice coach, the live audio session) is sent to our servers and to Google Gemini so the app can tutor, grade a borderline answer, or role-play an interview.
- We do not sell recordings, and we do not use the microphone for advertising.

## Cloud AI (optional)

Cloud AI is off until you turn it on in Settings. A separate switch controls Cloud AI for N-400 practice.

When Cloud AI is on, the app may send question text, your answers, chat messages, and interview transcripts to Firebase (Google Cloud) and Google Gemini to generate practice explanations, follow-up questions, or grading comments. Generated explanations may be cached by question and language so the same explanation can be reused.

AI output is practice help only. It is not legal advice, not an official USCIS result, and not a decision about eligibility or an immigration case.

## Analytics and crash reports

- **Analytics:** the app can send anonymous usage events (such as which screen was opened) to Google Analytics for Firebase, including a device or app-instance identifier. You can turn this off in Settings → "Opt out of anonymous analytics."
- **Crash reports:** if the app crashes, a report (stack trace, device model, OS version, and app version) is sent to Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the crash.

We do not use this information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

## Purchases

Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your card number or full payment details. When subscriptions are available, Google Play and our subscription provider (RevenueCat) tell the app whether your premium entitlement is active, which product you bought, and when it renews or expires. Prices shown in the app are for review until purchases are turned on.

## What we do not collect

The app does not request your location, contacts, photos, SMS, or call log. It does not ask for an A-Number or other immigration case identifier. It does not show third-party ads.

## Who receives information

| Recipient | What they receive | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Google Firebase | Account ID, cloud study and AI cache data, analytics events if not opted out, crash reports | Sign-in, storage, app configuration, diagnostics |
| Google (Sign-In, speech, Gemini) | Google account details if you sign in; speech or text you submit to a voice or Cloud AI feature | Sign-in, speech recognition, AI tutoring |
| Google Play | Purchase and subscription status | Billing, when purchases are enabled |
| RevenueCat | App user ID and entitlement status | Confirming premium access, when purchases are enabled |

These providers process data under their own privacy policies. We do not sell personal information.

## Children

The app is for adults preparing for naturalization (18+). It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## How long we keep information

- On-device data stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.
- Cloud account data stays until you delete the account, as described below.
- Cached AI explanations are kept so repeat questions can be answered without calling the model again, and are removed when the related account data is deleted.
- Crash and analytics data are kept according to Google Firebase retention settings.

## Your choices and deletion

- **Turn off Cloud AI:** Settings. Cloud AI and N-400 Cloud AI are separate switches.
- **Turn off analytics:** Settings → Opt out of anonymous analytics.
- **Delete data on this device:** open Privacy in the app → Delete all local data.
- **Delete the cloud account and cloud data:** Settings → Delete account & cloud data. This asks our server to delete the Firebase account data tied to you and then signs you out.
- Uninstalling the app removes the local database from the device.

## Security

Study data on the device uses the app's private storage. N-400 practice answers are encrypted on the device. Cloud requests are sent over HTTPS to Firebase. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. The effective date at the top will change, and the app's Privacy Policy link will point to the updated page.
